Wally Farms

A Farm of the Future

A 1,000+ acre climate-action farm in the Hudson Valley — home to independent farm businesses that share equipment, infrastructure and know-how.

Pulling the future forward

Wally Farms pairs time-tested farm practices and land stewardship with technological tools for a low-carbon future — tools that also ease the hard labor for our farmers.
